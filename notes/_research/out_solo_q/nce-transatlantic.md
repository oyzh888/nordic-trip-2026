# nce-transatlantic

**Q:** Flying from Nice (NCE) to San Francisco (SFO) or San Jose (SJC) in October 2026: are there any nonstop flights from Nice to the US west coast? What are the realistic one-connection routings (via Paris CDG, London Heathrow, Frankfurt, Amsterdam, Zurich), typical total travel time, and which airline alliances serve it? Does departing Nice in the morning allow same-day arrival in California?

## Flying from the French Riviera to the Golden State: A Look at Your Travel Options in October 2026

**While nonstop flights from Nice (NCE) to the US West Coast, including San Francisco (SFO) and San Jose (SJC), are not currently scheduled for October 2026, travelers have a variety of one-connection options through major European hubs. A morning departure from Nice can indeed allow for a same-day arrival in California, making the journey more convenient.**

There are no direct flights from Nice to San Francisco or San Jose. The previously operated seasonal direct route from Nice to Los Angeles (LAX) is also currently inactive. However, several airlines offer one-stop itineraries, connecting through key European airports.

### One-Connection Routings and Travel Times

For travelers heading to **San Francisco (SFO)**, common and realistic one-connection routings are available through the following major European airports:

*   **Paris (CDG):** A popular option, especially with Air France.
*   **London (LHR):** British Airways and other carriers offer connections through Heathrow.
*   **Frankfurt (FRA):** Lufthansa provides seamless connections through its main hub.
*   **Amsterdam (AMS):** KLM offers convenient one-stop flights.
*   **Zurich (ZRH):** Swiss International Air Lines connects passengers through Zurich.

The typical total travel time for these one-connection flights to San Francisco ranges from approximately **14 to 16 hours**, including the layover.

For those flying into **San Jose (SJC)**, one-connection flights are also the standard. While specific popular connection points are less frequently highlighted in flight searches, options are available through similar major European and even some North American hubs. The total travel time for flights to San Jose tends to be slightly longer, generally ranging from **17 to 22 hours**.

### Airline Alliances Serving the Routes

Travelers can choose from all three major airline alliances for their journey from Nice to the San Francisco Bay Area:

*   **Star Alliance:** This alliance offers a strong presence with airlines like **Lufthansa** (via Frankfurt), **SWISS** (via Zurich), and **United Airlines** (often in codeshare with its European partners).
*   **SkyTeam:** **Air France** (via Paris) and **Delta Air Lines** are key players in this alliance, providing numerous one-stop options.
*   **Oneworld:** **British Airways** (via London) and **American Airlines** offer competitive one-connection itineraries.

### Same-Day Arrival in California

Yes, a morning departure from Nice makes a same-day arrival in California possible. Given the significant time difference (California is typically 9 hours behind Nice), flights departing Nice in the morning will land in San Francisco or San Jose in the afternoon or evening of the same day. For example, a flight departing Nice at 8:00 AM local time with a total travel time of 15 hours would arrive in California around 5:00 PM local time on the same day. Several airlines offer early morning departures from Nice that facilitate this.
