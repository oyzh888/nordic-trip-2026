### In-depth Property Analysis

*   **Eliassen Rorbuer (Hamnøy):** While perfectly located and iconic, their two-bedroom cottages typically come with only one bathroom. They offer the quintessential rorbuer experience with stunning views.

*   **Reinefjorden Sjohus (Hamnøy):** These modern sea houses are in a prime location. Their larger units may have two bathrooms, but this is not explicitly stated on their website, so direct contact is recommended.

*   **Sakrisoy Rorbuer (Sakrisøy):** Famous for their yellow cabins, they offer a range of accommodations. For their larger cabins suitable for four, it is best to inquire directly about the number of bathrooms.

*   **Lofoten Planet BoutiqueHotel (Sørvågen):** This hotel offers a "Lofoten Planet Suite" which has two bedrooms, a private living room, kitchen, and importantly, a spacious high-quality bathroom, which seems to be a single bathroom. It's worth clarifying with them if they have any two-bathroom options.

*   **Hattvika Lodge (Ballstad):** This property explicitly offers modern cabins with two bathrooms, making it a strong contender for your needs. It is centrally located for exploring the islands, though a bit further from Reine.

*   **Nusfjord Arctic Resort (Nusfjord):** A beautifully preserved fishing village turned resort. While they have various cabins, information on two-bathroom units is not readily available on their website. Given the premium nature of the resort, it's worth an inquiry.

*   **Svinøya Rorbuer (Svolvær):** This is an excellent option as they offer several types of rorbuer with two bathrooms, such as the "Rorbu M+" and "Rorbu L+". They also have larger units with even more bathrooms. It is, however, the furthest from Reinebringen.

*   **Thon Hotel Lofoten (Svolvær) & Scandic Leknes (Leknes):** As traditional hotels, their standard and family rooms will have one bathroom. While they may have larger suites, these typically do not feature two full bathrooms. They also lack the self-catering kitchen facilities you desire.

### Airbnb vs. Hotel in October: Which is Better Value?

*   **Airbnb/Self-Catering Cabins:** For a group of four, an Airbnb or a self-catering cabin with a kitchen is likely to be better value than a hotel. The ability to prepare your own meals can lead to significant savings, as dining out in Norway can be expensive. These options also typically offer more space and a more "local" experience.

*   **Hotels:** Hotels offer the convenience of daily housekeeping, reception services, and often include a substantial breakfast buffet, which can be a great start to a day of hiking. For shorter stays, the convenience might outweigh the higher cost. In October, some hotels may offer lower rates after the peak season.

**Recommendation:** For your group of four wanting a self-contained unit, an Airbnb or a rorbu/cabin from a provider like Hattvika Lodge or Svinøya Rorbuer will likely offer the best combination of space, amenities (kitchen and two bathrooms), and overall value.

### Final Recommendations

Given your specific needs, **Hattvika Lodge** in Ballstad and **Svinøya Rorbuer** in Svolvær are the most promising of the listed properties, as they are confirmed to offer accommodations with two bathrooms and kitchens.

Between the two, Hattvika Lodge offers a more central location for accessing both the southern sights like Reine and the northern areas. Svinøya Rorbuer is ideal if you plan to spend more time around Svolvær and Henningsvær.
