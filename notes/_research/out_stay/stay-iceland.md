# stay-iceland

**Q:** Where to stay in Iceland on the nights of 25, 26, 27 and 28 September 2026 for a group of 4 adults, self-drive, wanting clean self-contained apartments/houses with TWO bathrooms, budget 250-500 EUR per unit per night: (a) 25 Sep Reykjavik city centre or old harbour, (b) 26 Sep near Vik i Myrdal or Hvolsvollur on the south coast, (c) 27 Sep near Jokulsarlon glacier lagoon / Hofn / Skaftafell, (d) 28 Sep near Keflavik airport or Reykjavik after an 18:00 Blue Lagoon visit. Give named specific properties with real booking URLs and typical nightly prices, and state how many bathrooms. Include both hotels (Exeter Hotel, Center Hotels Plaza, Reykjavik Residence, Hotel Vik i Myrdal, Black Beach Suites, Hotel Hvolsvollur, Fosshotel Glacier Lagoon, Hali Country Hotel, Hotel Klaustur, Aurora Hotel at KEF, Courtyard Keflavik) and apartment/guesthouse alternatives. Also: is the accommodation around Jokulsarlon very limited and expensive - what are the realistic alternatives and how far are they to drive? Is Airbnb or hotel better value in Iceland in late September?

Here is a guide to help you plan your self-drive trip to Iceland for a group of four adults from September 25th to 28th, 2026, with a focus on clean, self-contained apartments or houses with two bathrooms and a budget of €250-€500 per unit per night.

Please note that booking accommodation for September 2026 is not yet possible on most platforms. The prices mentioned are typical and based on current information, but are subject to change. It is recommended to book as soon as availability opens, usually 12-18 months in advance, especially for the popular South Coast.

### **Night of September 25th, 2026: Reykjavik City Centre or Old Harbour**

For your first night in Reykjavik, finding a self-contained apartment with two bathrooms is the most likely way to meet your group's needs. While some hotels offer suites, they often come with only one bathroom and may exceed your budget.

**Apartment & Guesthouse Alternatives:**

*   **Reykjavik Residence Apartment Hotel:** This hotel specializes in apartments and offers "Two-Bedroom, Two-Bathroom Apartments". These are an excellent option, combining the space and amenities of an apartment with hotel services. They are centrally located, and past guest reviews are generally very positive, highlighting the spacious and well-equipped rooms.
    *   **Booking:** You can find them on their official website: [https://www.rekhotels.is/reykjavik-residence-hotel](https://www.rekhotels.is/reykjavik-residence-hotel) or on booking platforms like Booking.com and Expedia.
    *   **Typical Price:** While a precise price for September 2026 is unavailable, similar apartments can range from €400-€600 per night, so it's worth checking as soon as booking opens.

*   **Vrbo & Airbnb:** These platforms are excellent resources for finding multi-bedroom and multi-bathroom apartments in Reykjavik.
    *   **Examples of what to look for:**
        *   A "Luxury apartment in central Reykjavik 2 bedroom and 2 bathrooms, keyless entry" has been listed on Vrbo.
        *   Another Vrbo listing mentions a "Premium Central Apartment with 2 bathrooms and private parking".
        *   On Airbnb, you can find listings like a "Modern 4 bdr, 3 bath Parliament Square Apartment".
    *   **Booking:** You will need to search these platforms closer to your travel date. Use the filters to select for "2+ bathrooms".
    *   **Typical Price:** Prices can vary widely, but you should be able to find options within your €250-€500 budget, especially if you book in advance.

**Hotel Alternatives (less likely to have two bathrooms in your budget):**

*   **Exeter Hotel:** Located in the Old Harbour, this is a contemporary design hotel. While they offer suites, these typically accommodate up to 3 guests and have one private bathroom. It's a great option for its location and style, but may not meet your bathroom requirements for a group of four.
*   **Center Hotels Plaza:** Situated in the heart of Reykjavik, this hotel offers a range of rooms. However, their rooms generally accommodate 2-3 guests and have one bathroom. Family rooms are available but are unlikely to have two bathrooms.

### **Night of September 26th, 2026: Near Vik i Myrdal or Hvolsvollur**

This area is a popular stop on the South Coast, and booking well in advance is crucial. Again, apartments or cottages will be your best bet for two bathrooms.

**Apartment & Guesthouse Alternatives:**

    *   **Booking:** You can find them on their website and major booking sites.
*   **Airbnb and Booking.com near Hvolsvollur and Vik:** These platforms have numerous cottages and holiday homes in the area.
    *   **Examples of what to look for:**

### **Night of September 27th, 2026: Near Jökulsárlón Glacier Lagoon / Höfn / Skaftafell**

This is the most challenging part of your accommodation search. **Yes, accommodation around Jökulsárlón is very limited and expensive.** There are very few options in the immediate vicinity of the lagoon. It is highly recommended to book this night's stay as soon as you confirm your travel dates.

**Realistic Alternatives and Driving Distances:**

*   **Hali:** The closest option to Jökulsárlón.
    *   **Hali Country Hotel:** Located about a 15-minute drive from Jökulsárlón, this hotel offers two-bedroom apartments and cottages that can sleep up to five people. While it's not explicitly stated that these have two bathrooms, it's worth inquiring directly. They have a restaurant on-site, which is convenient in this remote area.
        *   **Booking:** [https://hali.is/](https://hali.is/) and on booking sites.
        *   **Typical Price:** Prices for rooms are available on their website for 2025, with a two-bedroom apartment likely falling in the upper end or slightly above your budget.

*   **Skaftafell:** Located about a 45-50 minute drive west of Jökulsárlón.
    *   **Hotel Skaftafell:** A simple hotel with a prime location for exploring the Skaftafell Nature Reserve. It's unlikely to have two-bathroom units.
    *   Accommodation here is also limited.

*   **Höfn:** A fishing town about a 1-hour drive east of Jökulsárlón. This is your most realistic option for finding a wider range of accommodations, including apartments with multiple bathrooms, and services like supermarkets and restaurants.
    *   **Apartment & Guesthouse Alternatives in and around Höfn:**
        *   **Hvammur Apartments by the Harbour:** This property has a two-bedroom apartment with a sea view that includes a living room and a fully equipped kitchen. It's worth checking the bathroom situation.
        *   **Erobik Apartments:** These apartments are well-regarded, and some reviews mention a large bathroom. They offer two-bedroom units.
        *   **Airbnb and Booking.com:** You will find several apartment and house rentals in and around Höfn. Look for listings like "Modern one-bedroom apartment" that can sleep four and may have more than one bathroom.
    *   **Booking:** Search on booking platforms for apartments in Höfn for your dates.

**Hotel Alternatives (less likely to have two bathrooms):**

*   **Fosshotel Glacier Lagoon:** A modern and stylish hotel located about a 30-minute drive west of Jökulsárlón. While a luxurious option, their standard rooms and suites are unlikely to have two bathrooms.
*   **Hotel Klaustur (in Kirkjubæjarklaustur):** This is further away, about a 1 hour and 40-minute drive from Jökulsárlón. It's a viable option if you can't find anything closer.

Your 18:00 Blue Lagoon visit means you'll be looking for accommodation later in the evening. Staying near the airport is convenient for a morning departure.

**Apartment & Guesthouse Alternatives:**

    *   **Examples of what to look for:**

        *   Another Airbnb, a "family home in the heart of Keflavik", is also a good possibility.
        *   A recently renovated "Spacious and modern apartment" is also listed on Airbnb.
    *   **Booking:** Filter for your group size and "2+ bathrooms".

**Hotel Alternatives:**

*   **Aurora Hotel at Reykjavik-Keflavik Airport Terminal KEF:** This hotel is located directly at the airport terminal, offering maximum convenience. It's a good option for an early flight, but less likely to have two-bathroom suites within your budget.

### **Airbnb vs. Hotel in Iceland in Late September**

For a group of four, **Airbnb or other self-catering apartments generally offer better value than hotels in Iceland.**

**Pros of Airbnb/Apartments:**

*   **Cost-Effective:** For a group, renting a multi-bedroom apartment is often cheaper than booking two separate hotel rooms.
*   **Kitchen Facilities:** Having a kitchen allows you to prepare some of your own meals, which can lead to significant savings as eating out in Iceland is expensive.
*   **More Space and Privacy:** Apartments and houses provide more room to spread out and relax compared to a standard hotel room.
*   **Authentic Experience:** Staying in a residential area can offer a more local experience.
*   **Better Chance of Multiple Bathrooms:** It is much more common to find apartments and houses with two or more bathrooms than it is to find hotel rooms with this feature.

**Pros of Hotels:**

*   **Convenience:** Hotels offer services like 24-hour reception, on-site restaurants and bars, and daily housekeeping.
*   **Breakfast Included:** Many hotels include breakfast, which is a convenient way to start your day.
*   **Guaranteed Availability:** Hotels are often easier to book, especially for single nights, as some Airbnbs may have minimum stay requirements.

Given your specific need for two bathrooms and a desire for a self-contained unit, **apartments and houses from platforms like Airbnb, Vrbo, and Booking.com will likely be your best and most cost-effective options throughout your trip.**
