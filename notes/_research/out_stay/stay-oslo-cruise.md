### (2) Hurtigruten and Havila Coastal Ferry: Svolvær to Tromsø

The coastal ferry journey from Svolvær to Tromsø is a scenic overnight trip. Both Hurtigruten and Havila Voyages operate on this route. The typical schedule is a departure from Svolvær at 22:15 and arrival in Tromsø the next day at 14:15.

**Cabin Categories on Port-to-Port Fares:**

*   **Hurtigruten:** For port-to-port journeys, you can typically book **Polar Inside** (no window) and **Polar Outside** (with a window or porthole) cabins. Suites are generally not available for these shorter trips.
*   **Havila Voyages:** Havila offers a range of modern cabins, including interior and seaview options. They have recently restructured their cabin categories to be more descriptive.

**Typical Price for a 2-Berth Inside Cabin (in NOK):**

Prices are indicative for early October and can vary. It's recommended to check the official websites for exact pricing for your travel dates.

*   A 2-berth inside cabin on this route can range from approximately **1,400 to 2,100 NOK** per person.

**Shared/No-Cabin Option:**

*   Yes, for journeys under 23 hours, it is not mandatory to book a cabin on Hurtigruten. This means you can travel as a "deck passenger," utilizing the public lounges and seating areas. Havila also offers a seating-only option for budget-conscious travelers.

**Online Booking for Port-to-Port:**

*   Yes, both Hurtigruten and Havila allow you to book port-to-port journeys online through their official websites.

**Meal Inclusions:**

*   For port-to-port fares on both Hurtigruten and Havila, **meals are not included**. You can purchase meals and snacks from the onboard cafes and restaurants. Both lines offer the option to add meal packages during the booking process.

**Difference Between Hurtigruten and Havila on this Leg:**

While both companies sail the same route with a similar schedule, there are some key differences:

*   **Ship Modernity:** Havila's fleet is brand new, with ships launched in 2021 and later. They feature modern Scandinavian design and are equipped with large battery packs for quieter, emission-free sailing in sensitive areas. Hurtigruten's fleet consists of a variety of ships of different ages, some of which have been refurbished.
*   **Onboard Atmosphere:** Havila is often described as having a calm and peaceful atmosphere, focusing on the scenery rather than extensive entertainment. Hurtigruten carries a long heritage, having operated the coastal route since 1893.
*   **Dining:** Havila's dining is often served à la carte with table service, even for included meals on longer voyages. Hurtigruten's main restaurant typically offers a mix of buffets and set menus. For port-to-port passengers, both have cafes with various options.
*   **Sustainability:** Havila places a strong emphasis on sustainability with its modern, LNG and battery-powered ships.
