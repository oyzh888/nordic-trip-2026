# havila-10-02

**Q:** On Friday 2 October 2026, which coastal express ship (Hurtigruten or Havila Voyages) departs Svolvær northbound at about 22:15 and arrives Tromsø the next day around 14:15? Havila operates 4 ships (Capella, Castor, Polaris, Pollux) on the Bergen-Kirkenes route alongside Hurtigruten's 7. Give the operator, ship name if known, and explain how the 11-ship rotation makes daily departures. Cite sources.

On Friday, 2 October 2026, the coastal express ship departing from Svolvær at approximately 22:15 and arriving in Tromsø the following day around 14:15 will be operated by **Havila Voyages**.

The specific ship making this journey is scheduled to be the **Havila Polaris**. This determination is based on the vessel's departure from Bergen on Monday, 28 September 2026, which places it in Svolvær on the evening of 2 October, the fifth day of its northbound voyage.

### The Daily Departure and 11-Ship Rotation

The Norwegian Coastal Express route is a continuous service, with a ship departing Bergen every single day of the year. This remarkable feat is achieved through the coordinated operation of 11 ships in total. The service is a partnership between two companies:
*   **Hurtigruten**, which operates seven ships on the route.
*   **Havila Voyages**, which operates four new ships: Havila Capella, Havila Castor, Havila Polaris, and Havila Pollux.

The full round trip from Bergen to Kirkenes and back to Bergen takes 11 days to complete. With 11 ships in constant rotation, each vessel begins the journey from Bergen every 11 days. This staggered system ensures that on any given day, one ship is starting its northbound voyage from Bergen, while the other ten are at various other ports along the extensive Norwegian coastline. This allows the coastal service to function as both a reliable local ferry and a continuous cruise experience.
