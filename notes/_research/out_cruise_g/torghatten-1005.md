### Svolvær-Tromsø Express Boat (Hurtigbåt)

Based on available information, there is **no direct express boat (hurtigbåt) service** operating between Svolvær and Tromsø. The primary express boat in the Tromsø region, Route 2, runs between Tromsø, Finnsnes, and Harstad, but does not extend to Svolvær or the Lofoten Islands.

Travelers looking for a sea connection between Svolvær and Tromsø have the following alternatives:

*   **Hurtigruten/Havila Coastal Voyage:** Both Hurtigruten and Havila operate the coastal route, which includes stops in both Svolvær and Tromsø. This is not an express service but a larger car ferry that is part of a longer coastal voyage. The journey between Svolvær and Tromsø with these operators takes approximately 16 hours.
*   **Bus or Flight:** The most practical alternatives for traveling between Svolvær and Tromsø are typically by bus (which may involve transfers) or by flying.
*   **Express Boat to/from Bodø:** There is an express boat that connects Svolvær with Bodø, which is south of Lofoten. This does not serve the route to Tromsø.
