## Havila Voyages: Svolvær to Tromsø Overnight Trip Costs and Policies

For travelers looking to journey the roughly 16-hour overnight leg from Svolvær to Tromsø, Havila Voyages offers a "port-to-port" (havn-til-havn) option with several accommodation choices. While exact, current prices are dynamic and must be obtained directly through the Havila Voyages online booking portal, this report provides typical price estimates, meal information, and the cancellation policy for these shorter journeys.

On the Svolvær to Tromsø route, the ship typically departs Svolvær in the late evening and arrives in Tromsø the following afternoon, making for a journey of approximately 16 hours.

### **Accommodation and Estimated Prices**

Havila Voyages provides a range of options for port-to-port travelers, though specific pricing is not listed on a simple price sheet and varies by season and availability. Travelers can choose from the following, with price estimates gathered from third-party sources:

*   **Deck Space / Seat without Cabin:** This is the most budget-friendly option, providing access to a reclining chair for the overnight journey.
*   **Inside Cabin:** A private cabin without a window.
*   **Outside Cabin:** A private cabin with a window.
*   **Suites:** More luxurious and spacious cabins are also available.

While official prices are only available during booking, travel booking site Rome2Rio indicates that a ferry journey on this route with Havila can be estimated in the range of 800-950 NOK, although it is not specified what level of accommodation this includes.

### **Meals and Onboard Dining**

For port-to-port tickets, meals are not included in the fare. Passengers have the option to purchase meals and snacks individually at the onboard restaurants and cafés. The ships feature several dining venues:

*   **Havrand Restaurant:** The main dining hall serving breakfast, lunch, and dinner with an à la carte menu that changes to reflect the region the ship is sailing through.
*   **Havly Café:** A more casual option open for long hours, offering a variety of dishes like sandwiches, pizza, burgers, and traditional Norwegian fish soup. Prices at the café are listed for port-to-port passengers.
*   **Hildring Fine Dining:** An exclusive restaurant offering a five-course signature menu for an additional charge. A dinner here for those not on a full-board package costs 855 NOK.

While specific current prices for all meals were not available, a traveler in February 2024 reported that a dinner in the main restaurant for a port-to-port passenger cost 595 NOK.

### **Cancellation Policy for Port-to-Port Tickets**

The cancellation policy for port-to-port journeys depends on the type of ticket purchased. Havila Voyages offers different ticket types, such as a "FLEX-billett," which provides greater flexibility for changes and cancellations. For cancellations, fees may apply and will vary depending on how close to the departure date the cancellation is made. It is recommended to check the specific terms and conditions at the time of booking to understand the applicable rules. If a sailing is cancelled by Havila Voyages, passengers are typically offered the option to rebook on another voyage or receive a full refund.
