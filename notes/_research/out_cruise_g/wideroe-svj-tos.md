**Q:** What are typical one-way fares and flight times for Widerøe (or SAS/Norwegian) from Svolvær Helle (SVJ) or Leknes (LKN) to Tromsø (TOS) in early October? Include whether the route is direct or via Bodø, flight duration, and typical NOK price range. Cite sources.

## Flight Options from Lofoten to Tromsø in Early October

Travelers looking to fly from the Lofoten Islands to Tromsø in early October will primarily find flights operated by Widerøe, with both direct and connecting options available. SAS and Norwegian Air Shuttle also serve the region, but Widerøe is the main carrier for direct flights on these specific routes. October is generally considered the off-season, which can result in more affordable fares.

### From Svolvær Helle (SVJ) to Tromsø (TOS)

**Route and Duration:**
Widerøe operates both direct and indirect flights from Svolvær to Tromsø. A direct flight has an average duration of about 50 minutes. Indirect flights, which often include a layover in Bodø, will have a longer travel time. For instance, some connecting flights can take around 1 hour and 45 minutes.

**Typical Fares:**
One-way fares can vary significantly based on how far in advance you book and the flexibility of your travel dates.
*   Widerøe's website shows one-way prices for late September and early October ranging from approximately **1,819 NOK to 3,699 NOK**.
*   Other travel sites indicate that one-way fares can be found starting from $77 (approximately 718 NOK) to $107 (approximately 998 NOK). An indirect flight in early October was noted for $90 (approximately 840 NOK).

### From Leknes (LKN) to Tromsø (TOS)

**Route and Duration:**
Widerøe is the sole airline offering non-stop flights from Leknes to Tromsø. These direct flights have a duration of approximately 55 minutes. Connecting flights are also available.

**Typical Fares:**
As with the Svolvær route, prices for flights from Leknes can fluctuate. October is noted as the cheapest month to fly this route.
*   On the Widerøe website, one-way fares for late September and early October are listed from **1,999 NOK to 3,699 NOK**.
*   Various booking platforms show one-way prices for early October starting from $87 (approximately 812 NOK) to $107 (approximately 998 NOK). A direct one-way flight for October 13th was found for $101 (approximately 942 NOK).

*Note: Prices in NOK converted from USD are based on an exchange rate of 1 USD = 9.33 NOK and are approximate. Airfares are subject to change and availability.*
