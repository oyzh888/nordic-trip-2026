# lofoten-to-tromso-air

**Q:** What is the best way to fly from Lofoten (Svolvær SVJ or Leknes LKN) to Tromsø on a Friday in early October? Are there same-day connections via Bodø (BOO) with Widerøe/SAS, what are realistic departure/arrival times, total journey time, and typical total one-way fare in NOK for 4 adults? Also is there a direct Harstad/Narvik Evenes (EVE) to Tromsø flight?

### Flying from Lofoten to Tromsø: Your Options for a Friday in Early October

For a journey from the Lofoten Islands to Tromsø on a Friday in early October, you have several flight options from Svolvær (SVJ) and Leknes (LKN) airports. The primary airline for these routes is Widerøe, with SAS also operating some connecting flights.

#### **Same-Day Connections via Bodø (BOO) with Widerøe/SAS**

Yes, there are same-day connections available from both Svolvær and Leknes to Tromsø with a layover in Bodø.

*   **From Svolvær (SVJ) via Bodø (BOO):** You can find several flight combinations with Widerøe and SAS.
    *   **Realistic Departure/Arrival Times:** Expect morning and afternoon departures from Svolvær. The total journey time, including a layover in Bodø, typically ranges from **2 hours and 50 minutes to over 4 hours**. For example, a morning departure might get you to Tromsø in the early afternoon.
*   **From Leknes (LKN) via Bodø (BOO):** Similar to Svolvær, there are connecting flights available.
    *   **Realistic Departure/Arrival Times:** Departure times are often in the afternoon. The total travel time will be comparable to flying from Svolvær, with the layover in Bodø being the main variable.

#### **Direct Flights from Lofoten to Tromsø**

A more convenient and faster option is to take a direct flight with Widerøe.

*   **From Svolvær (SVJ) to Tromsø (TOS):**
    *   **Journey Time:** The direct flight duration is approximately **50 minutes**.
    *   **Realistic Departure/Arrival Times:** Widerøe has historically offered direct flights on Fridays, with schedules showing departures in the morning (e.g., around 9:05 AM) and mid-afternoon (e.g., around 3:45 PM).
*   **From Leknes (LKN) to Tromsø (TOS):**
    *   **Journey Time:** The direct flight takes about **55 minutes**.
    *   **Realistic Departure/Arrival Times:** These flights often depart in the afternoon, with past schedules showing departures around 3:40 PM or 6:05 PM. There are approximately 6 direct flights per week on this route.

#### **Typical Total One-Way Fare for 4 Adults in NOK**

Flight prices can vary significantly based on how far in advance you book and the demand for your travel dates. October is generally considered a more affordable month to fly compared to the peak summer season.

*   Based on available data, a one-way ticket per person can range from approximately 900 NOK to over 2,000 NOK.
*   For **four adults**, a realistic, though rough, estimate for a one-way journey would be in the range of **3,600 to 8,000 NOK**. Booking well in advance will likely secure prices at the lower end of this spectrum.

#### **Direct Flight from Harstad/Narvik Evenes (EVE) to Tromsø (TOS)**

Yes, there is a direct flight option from Harstad/Narvik Evenes Airport (EVE) to Tromsø, which can be a good alternative depending on your location in Lofoten.

*   **Airlines:** Both Widerøe and Norwegian operate on this route.
*   **Journey Time:** This is a very short flight, typically lasting about **40 minutes**.
*   **Frequency:** There are flights available multiple times a week.

### **Recommendation**

The **best way to fly** from Svolvær or Leknes to Tromsø is to take a **direct Widerøe flight**. This will be the fastest and most convenient option, with a travel time of under an hour. While connecting flights via Bodø offer more scheduling flexibility, they significantly increase the total journey time.

If traveling to Harstad/Narvik Evenes (EVE) is convenient, a direct flight from there is also an excellent and very quick option.

To get the most accurate schedule and pricing for your specific Friday in early October, it is highly recommended to check the official websites of **Widerøe** and **SAS** directly.
