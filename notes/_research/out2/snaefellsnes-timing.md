Your 05:30 start from Keflavik will involve driving in darkness for about the first hour.

Your hard deadline is dictated by evening flights to Oslo. Based on typical schedules, as the exact September 2026 schedule may not be finalized, here are your likely options. All flights depart from Keflavik (KEF). There are no direct flights from Reykjavik's domestic airport (RKV) to Oslo.

*   **SAS (Scandinavian Airlines)**: Typically has daily flights. Their evening service can be around **18:35 (6:35 PM)**.
*   **Icelandair**: Operates multiple daily flights. While many are in the morning, evening options can exist.
*   **Play Air**: You should check their schedule closer to the date as it can vary.

To be safe, you should aim to be at Keflavik Airport (KEF) **no later than 19:00 (7:00 PM)**, which allows for a flight departing around 20:00-21:00. If your flight is earlier, this timeline becomes significantly tighter.

#### 4. Road Conditions for Route 54

Route 54 is the main road on the southern part of the peninsula, while Route 574 (Útnesvegur) loops around the western tip through the Snæfellsjökull National Park.

*   **Paving Status**: The majority of the main loop (Road 54 and 574) is paved. However, there are still known **gravel sections**, particularly on the northern side of the peninsula (part of Route 54 east of Stykkishólmur) and around the westernmost tip.
*   **Vehicle Choice**: For late September, a standard 2WD vehicle is generally considered sufficient as long as the weather is good. However, you must slow down on gravel sections. A 4x4 is not mandatory but offers better handling on unpaved surfaces. Always check the live road status on [road.is](https://umferdin.is/en) before you depart.

**Yes, the day loop is feasible, but it will be a very long and rushed day.**

*   **09:20 - 09:50**: Arrive at **Búðir**. Spend 30 minutes at the black church and surroundings.
*   **10:10 - 11:40**: Arrive at **Arnarstapi/Hellnar**. Allow 1.5 hours for the cliff walk and a quick coffee. The walk between the two villages is a highlight.
*   **12:40 - 14:10**: Arrive at **Kirkjufell**. Allow 1.5 hours for photos at Kirkjufellsfoss and to enjoy the view. This also provides a buffer for lunch in nearby Grundarfjörður.
*   **14:10**: Depart from the Kirkjufell area.
*   **18:00 - 18:30**: Approximate arrival time at Keflavik Airport (KEF). This is a non-stop drive of approximately 3.5-4 hours from the Kirkjufell area back to the airport via the main roads.

This timeline gets you back to KEF between 18:00 and 18:30, leaving a 30-60 minute buffer before your 19:00 target.
