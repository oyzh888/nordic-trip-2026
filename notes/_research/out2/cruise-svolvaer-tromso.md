### 5. Availability and Fallback Options: Flights

The Svolvær to Tromsø coastal route is popular, especially during periods with high chances of seeing the Northern Lights, which includes early October. It is advisable to book in advance to secure your passage, particularly if you require cabins for four people.

Should the voyage be sold out, or if you require a faster mode of transport, Widerøe operates flights from both Svolvær (SVJ) and the nearby Leknes (LKN) airport to Tromsø (TOS).

*   **Widerøe Flights from Svolvær (SVJ) to Tromsø (TOS):**
    *   **Typical Times:** Widerøe offers direct flights with a duration of about 50 minutes. Flight schedules vary, but there are often several departures throughout the day.
    *   **Typical Price:** One-way tickets can be found for as low as $77-$107 (approximately 800-1100 NOK), but prices fluctuate based on demand and how far in advance you book.
*   **Widerøe Flights from Leknes (LKN) to Tromsø (TOS):**
    *   **Typical Times:** Direct flights from Leknes to Tromsø take about 55 minutes. There are typically 6 flights per week on this route.
    *   **Typical Price:** Prices are comparable to flights from Svolvær, with one-way fares starting from around $84-$107 (approximately 870-1100 NOK).

### 6. Hurtigruten vs. Havila: The Onboard Experience

While both companies ply the same scenic route, there are notable differences between their fleets and onboard ambiance.

*   **Hurtigruten:** With a history dating back to 1893, Hurtigruten's ships have a more traditional and classic feel. They have long served as a vital link for cargo and local passengers, which contributes to an authentic "working ship" atmosphere. The fleet consists of a variety of ships, each with its own character.
*   **Havila Voyages:** A newer entrant, Havila began service in 2021 with a fleet of brand-new, modern ships. These vessels are designed with a focus on sustainability, featuring large battery packs and running on LNG, which allows for silent, emission-free sailing in sensitive areas like fjords. The cabins on Havila's ships are generally larger and have a more contemporary Scandinavian design. In terms of dining, Havila has moved away from traditional buffets for lunch and dinner, instead offering an à la carte menu with small, made-to-order dishes to reduce food waste.

Ultimately, the choice between Hurtigruten and Havila may come down to your preference for a traditional maritime experience versus a modern, eco-conscious journey.
